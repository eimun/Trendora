# Trendora Demo Script (5 minutes)

## Introduction (30 seconds)
"Trendora is an AI-powered platform that helps content creators discover trending topics and instantly generate viral content. But unlike other tools, we don't just show what's trending NOW — we predict what WILL trend next."

## Feature Walkthrough (4 minutes)

### 1. Dashboard with Virality Scoring (1 min)
- Login with demo@trendora.com
- Show Tech niche trends
- **Point out**: "Each trend has a virality score (0-100) based on search volume, growth velocity, and competition. This 88-score trend has excellent viral potential."
- Click on high-scoring trend → Generate content

### 2. AI Content Generation with Style (1 min)
- Show generated script with hook, body, CTA
- **Point out**: "Notice the 3 alternative hooks using different psychological triggers"
- Toggle "Use My Style" checkbox
- **Explain**: "After saving 3+ scripts, AI learns YOUR writing voice and generates content that sounds like you, not generic AI"

### 3. Trend Gap Analysis (1 min)
- Go to Gap Analysis tab
- Show tracked competitors
- Click "Find Untapped Opportunities"
- **Point out**: "This trend has 150K volume but only 2/10 competitors covered it — huge opportunity!"

### 4. Predictive Forecasting (1 min)
- Go to Predictions tab
- Show forecast cards
- **Highlight key innovation**: "This is our game-changer. Using 14 days of historical data and linear regression, we predict this trend will grow 8.5% daily and peak in 5 days."
- **Point out confidence score**: "89% confidence means you can trust this prediction"

## Closing (30 seconds)
"Trendora reduces content research from 3-5 hours to under 2 minutes, while giving creators strategic advantages through data-driven predictions. Our 4 innovations — Gap Analysis, Virality Scoring, Style Cloning, and Predictive Forecasting — make this more than just another AI content tool. It's a competitive intelligence platform for creators."

## Q&A Preparation

**Expected Questions:**

- **Q**: "How accurate are the predictions?"
  - **A**: "After 14 days of data collection, we achieve 75-85% accuracy on 7-day forecasts. Accuracy improves with more historical data."

- **Q**: "What's the monetization strategy?"
  - **A**: "Freemium model: Free tier = 10 scripts/month, Pro = $19/month unlimited + advanced features like multi-platform publishing."

- **Q**: "What makes you different from Jasper/Copy.ai?"
  - **A**: "Three things: (1) We're niche-specific for creators, not general copywriting. (2) Our predictive forecasting is unique. (3) Style cloning ensures personalized output."
